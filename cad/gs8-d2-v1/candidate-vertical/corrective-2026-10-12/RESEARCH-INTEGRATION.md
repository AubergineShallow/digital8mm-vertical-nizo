# D2 corrective review research applicability

The materials, ribbons and cooler research informs the 12 October corrective review. It does not establish mechanical strength, cooler fit, useful airflow, thermal performance or ribbon life. The preserved design baseline is `bdfd44fc549bd8ea9ebf9a93ea4262ad5054b13f`. The research files describe that baseline; numerical geometry claims must come from the subsequently selected saved CAD and its current reports.

The source documents are [materials and ribbon evidence](materials-and-ribbons-baseline-evidence.txt), its [PDF copy](materials-and-ribbons-baseline-evidence.pdf), and [cooler fit and thermal evidence](cooler-baseline-evidence.docx). Their baseline findings are retained rather than silently rewritten to describe a later design. No physical D2 test or manufacturer contact was performed for those reports.

## Material density and strength

The inherited model uses 1.07 g/cm³ ASA and a 0.85 empirical printed-mass factor. [Polymaker's ASA technical data](https://wiki.polymaker.com/polymaker-products/more-about-our-products/documents/technical-data-sheets/abs-asa/polymaker-tm-asa) gives 1.13 g/cm³. Holding geometry and that factor fixed, `(1.13 / 1.07 - 1) × 100 = 5.6075%`: about **+5.6% of the ASA contribution**. This is not a 5.6% increase in complete-camera mass. It does not apply to TPU or purchased parts, select an exact grade, or silently change any manifest mass. The structural supplement exposes this sensitivity separately from its inherited mass calculation.

The current Polymaker test data uses conditions different from D2's proposed shell/infill process. Its XY/Z tensile figures, older Polymaker data and Prusament results must not be pooled into one design allowable. Layer adhesion, print direction, drying, chamber conditions, voids, notches and warm creep remain specific to the actual process. Published HDT is not a continuous loaded-service temperature. Retain the E=0.8/1.6/2.4 GPa and 5/10/20/30 MPa scenarios as sensitivity cases only. Exact filament grade, colour, lot and qualified allowables remain unknown. Weigh the finished prints and harness before final mass, centre-of-mass and load qualification.

## Bonded catch

The research describes the baseline tenon at about 3.1 × 1.6 mm with 3.5 mm engagement. Those dimensions are historical inputs, not measurements of a corrective export. The selected corrective source uses deeper bonded engagement while keeping the existing datum-tilt test and catch-window acceptance. The selected manifest exposes the actual engagement, section, pocket clearances and ceiling through `tab_catch_joint`; its structural supplement repeats those bound fields. Read the selected CAD, current main audit and [fix log](../review-2026-10-10/FIX-LOG.md) for the implemented dimensions and outcome.

[3M's bonded-joint guidance](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1965232O/3ms-structural-two-part-epoxy-adhesive-whitepaper.pdf) supports spreading loads and avoiding concentrated peel or cleavage. It supplies no allowable torque for this printed catch. [Henkel's plastics guide](https://www.ellsworth.com/globalassets/literature-library/manufacturer/henkel-loctite/henkel-loctite-design-guide-plastic-bonding.pdf) and the [published printed-ASA experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijadhadh.2020.102602) concern different material/process/joint conditions. They do not authorize an epoxy or cyanoacrylate substitution or generic preparation rule.

The exact epoxy SKU, mixing and cure schedule, preparation, gap filling and environmental durability remain unselected. A [source-bound two-piece local study](../structural-review/bonded-catch-coupon/README.md) now supplies the exact production insert and a crop of the host print stock in their production orientations. Its geometry checks do not qualify the bond. Qualify the real stub and pocket, including worst measured gaps and tilt. Use the inherited 0.5 N·m design-torque screen only after fixture/protocol review; document deflection, permanent position change and failure mode, including after relevant hot dwell. A deeper tenon or passing geometric window does not prove bond strength, long-term creep or release for assembly. The proposed research sample counts and test margins are engineering choices, not supplier ratings or certification requirements.

## Cable routing and assembly

[Raspberry Pi's camera connection instructions](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/accessories/camera.html) call for straight seating, correct contacts and careful latch operation, warn against sharp-angle bending, and recommend installing the camera cable before the Active Cooler. That sequence must inform the assembly guide and access review; a static cable envelope cannot prove latch manipulation after assembly.

[Adafruit 3562](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3562) is an intact 300 × 10 mm ribbon. Its product documentation warns that the DIY system is less durable and less shielded than ready-made cables. [Hicenda's HMX039-V1 page](https://www.hicenda.com/product/Microoled-display-1024x768-high-resolution-microdisplay.html) does not supply a numeric flex bend-radius or cycle rating. The baseline research found no applicable numeric minimum radius or cycle limit for the actual CSI, HDMI or OLED flex. **Those three ratings remain unknown.** Bend figures from unrelated Molex cable series are examples of construction dependence, not substitute limits.

Keep measured width, thickness, stiffeners, contact side, connector exits, assembly sweep and service sweep separate from a guessed round cable envelope. A thinner ribbon model can remove a false collision without establishing electrical performance. Verify strain relief, stored length, continuity and actual camera/HDMI modes across the intended assembly/service cycles. No new cable or connector purchase is authorized by this note. Where existing wire length, resistance and reach requirements conflict, preserve the failed result and propose a bounded decision separately.

## Cooler and airflow

The [official Active Cooler drawing](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/cooling/raspberry-pi-active-cooler-mechanical-drawing.pdf) gives an approximate 63.50 × 42.50 × 13.70 mm envelope and warns that it is not production data. The [product brief](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/cooling/raspberry-pi-active-cooler-product-brief.pdf) establishes the mounting method and fan specification. A centred envelope, coarse proxy or free-area pass cannot establish the installed transform, pin/latch space, lead exit or service sweep. No applicable manufacturer pressure-flow curve or complete lead geometry was established in the research.

[Manufacturer thermal tests](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/heating-and-cooling-raspberry-pi-5/) and the research's independent tests concern other enclosures, workloads and fan-control revisions. They support using the official cooler as a candidate; their temperatures are not a D2 forecast. The baseline report's hot minimum cut of 444.5 mm², 493.2 mm² empty-bay bound and 510 mm² ports are explicitly historical. The corrective outlet work remains within the existing envelope, but only a saved-export report can establish its new geometric results.

The structural PDF now reads mouth necks from `shadow-plenum-checks.json` and path/series results from `check-scope-checks.json`. These are geometric section areas and graph capacities. A passing geometric area rule does not measure installed airflow, pressure drop, recirculation or temperature. The unsealed CSI opening and internal bypass remain relevant. Keep unobstructed, hand, strap and stand/support occlusion cases distinct; a blocked underside mouth cannot receive credit for its unavailable opening area.

Use paired open/closed camera tests at measured ambient with actual capture, preview and sustained recording workload. Log SoC temperature/clock/throttle/undervoltage, fan state, SSD temperature and recording errors, and measure battery/UPS/internal air separately. Record software and fan settings. The actual battery and SSD limits, exact cooler registration, complete harness, warm adhesive behaviour and closed-body thermal evidence remain open.

## Evidence sequencing and release state

The evidence refresh order is saved geometry and manifest, plenum checks, three plenum crops and receipts, structural calculation, check-scope report, then the structural PDF. The PDF binds all those inputs and the research files in its receipt. This order avoids a circular dependency because check-scope consumes the structural J4 load screen.

There are nine printed assembly rows in the compact model but ten physical production prints: the tub body and bonded catch stub are separate prints. The original-storage variant adds one sleeve, giving ten assembly rows and eleven physical prints. The centring gauge, three plenum coupons, nine retained structural coupon pieces and additional two-piece local bonded-catch study are excluded from those production counts.

All applicable inherited physical gates and M1 remain open. The complete camera, bond, cooling path and flexible service remain unqualified. A refreshed PDF receipt confirms document identity and review, not hardware release.
