# D2 custom structural coupon kit

Concealed-vent update: these nine coupon geometries are retained from the reviewed structural study and do not represent the new concealed passages. Read `../CONCEALED-VENT-STRUCTURAL.md` and its bound JSON for the revised assembly mass and J4 load target. The original 0.90 N·m target below is historical; it must not be applied as a current load bound. A representative vent-section coupon, slicer inspection and whole-body warm/handling tests remain required. No coupon has been printed or tested.

Nine custom pieces support comparative structural screening. They are not ASTM/ISO test specimens, production parts, print-ready approvals or physical pass results. Source STEP stays in coupon/source coordinates; STL is rotated and translated to the declared print pose. The coupon manifest gives dimensions, coordinate mapping, source hashes and fixture/load points. Verify slicer toolpaths, supports, dimensions and all loading fixtures before testing.

## Purpose and setup

- C01 and C02 compare the nominal old 4 ×3 mm and reinforced 8 ×5 mm carrier arm. Clamp only X−15..0, load at local X=37.5766 mm in local Z. The beam extends another 5 mm past the load point. The oversized clamp block is a fixture aid; these coupons omit the real ear/root/frame and therefore cannot qualify the assembled carrier.
- C03 and C04 compare the heel bridge at 2.5 and 5.5 mm wall thickness. Their layer normal is local X, matching assembly Z. Print in the supplied sideways pose with a suitable brim; the narrow bed contact needs review. Do not rotate flat for convenience, because that changes the tested layer direction.
- C05 and C06 compare the upper bridge width 2 and 4 mm. Layer normal is local Z. The 13 mm span and two 3.5 mm slots are custom idealisations. Clamp the outer frame beyond the span; load the bridge in local Z. Compare a central pusher with the actual 12 mm webbing. The local frame does not reproduce the complete grip/wrap route.
- C07 represents a diameter 9 mm carrier boss, 4 mm total thickness, diameter 2.8 mm through-hole and diameter 5.3 mm by 1 mm counterbore, leaving 3 mm below the head. Use the actual M2.5 screw and standoff with controlled reaction/fixture contact. Separate compression/creep from tab-supported pull-through. Do not infer preload from torque alone, and do not transfer coupon torque to production without review.
- C08 and C09 are actual selected 2.4 mm lip/head trial STEP extracts of the front J4 base and tongue. Print in their production directions, assemble through the real entry window and slide by 10 mm. Use an external rigid fixture; do not clamp the lips themselves. They omit the rear joint, independent lock and full body, so they only screen local mating surfaces. Verify the remaining truncated edges do not control failure. The two components have different print poses and must be returned to source assembly coordinates to mate.

The arm pair prints normal to local Z (equivalent to assembly Y). Heel coupons print normal to local X (equivalent to assembly Z). Upper coupons print normal to local Z (equivalent to assembly Z). The seat prints with its local +Z head/counterbore face down (equivalent to production +Y face down). Verify the first-layer opening and counterbore dimension after printing. J4 coupons preserve their original part orientations. Brims/supports are process aids and must be removed without changing loaded surfaces; the J4 tongue may need support in the same undercut zones as production. No slicer has been run.

## Small comparison matrix

1. Start with the existing 0.20 mm process and the current part orientation. Record actual brand/lot, nozzle/line width, wall count, temperatures, chamber, speed/fan, drying and conditioning.
2. For material-direction evidence, use a qualified laboratory's tensile geometry at 0.16, 0.20 and 0.24 mm, at least five replicates per height in each of two principal load directions (30 samples minimum). These custom coupons do not replace tensile standards.
3. For the changed sections, print at least three replicates of C01–C07 at 0.20 mm with dense critical sections. Use the same nozzle, line width, solid local region, conditioning and load direction when comparing old/new geometry. If a layer-height candidate beats the baseline in material coupons, repeat the relevant old/new pair at that height. Do not change height, geometry and infill together and attribute the result to height.
4. Use at least three C08/C09 paired sets at 0.20 mm to screen J4 pull/rock and fit in the actual orientations. Keep any 0.20 mm sacrificial membranes elsewhere in the design separately verified if a different layer height is chosen.
5. The geometry-only meshes all passed closure, winding, signed-volume, STEP-volume and bed-placement checks. This says nothing about process strength or test performance.

## Load steps and records

- Arm coupons: staged 0.6, 1.8 and 3.0 N at the marked coordinate, 60 s hold, then unload; record peak displacement, 60 s displacement and residual after 60 s. Stop at visible damage, fixture slip or large nonlinear motion. The old arm may exceed the small-deflection range; this is a finding, not permission to rely on the linear number.
- Strap bridges: staged 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 N using a shielded fixture and inert samples. Record load distribution (pusher/webbing), direction and span. The 100 N case is a proposed screen, not a product rating. A fractured coupon is destructive evidence, not a released design.
- Seat: first record head and standoff contact diameters and thread engagement. Use an instrumented axial-force sweep if available; otherwise log applied torque and displacement without claiming the clamp force. Test creep/relaxation after 1 h and 24 h and at a temperature justified by the measured camera operating condition. No blanket assembly torque is specified here.
- J4 pair, historical proposal: incremental pull to 10, 20 and 40 N; the earlier study proposed rocking up to 0.90 N m using a fixture with a measured lever arm. The historical 0.90 N m case covered nominal equal sharing plus the stated wiring-location sensitivity for the earlier mass; it does not bound 70% reaction at one joint. These are test proposals, not whole-joint ratings. Record opening, slip, cracks and residual displacement. Replace fixtures/coupons if clamping damage affects the result.

Use the report's editable record sheet and test-record-template.json. Record failures and stopped tests, not only surviving samples. Protect eyes/hands, restrain broken fragments and keep live battery/electronics out of destructive fixtures. Stop if the setup cannot safely apply or measure the intended load. Keep PT reuse within the baseline five-drive limit; repeated tests beyond that require appropriate fresh samples or a separately qualified joint.

## Reproduce retained coupon geometry

The minimal original generator, mesh checker, optional renderer, selected J4 STEP pair and optional inherited comparator inputs are restored unchanged in the parent structural-review directory. Their hashes match the recovered archive; see [the restoration receipt](../coupon-reproducer-recovery.json). Retained coupon STEP/STL files also match their existing manifest. This restores reproduction sources without regenerating or overwriting the historical outputs. Old numerical load targets remain historical; use [the current structural supplement](../CONCEALED-VENT-STRUCTURAL.md).

To preserve the retained output snapshot, reproduce in a copied structural-review workspace. Run make_coupons.py in that structural-review folder with the repository's pinned CadQuery interpreter. It uses the copied j4-trial STEP for C08/C09; the inherited comparator remains in input-baseline and the original study archive contains its earlier analytical screening, while explicit parameters for the other coupons are in the restored generator. Run check_coupon_meshes.py with standard Python to check every generated STL. The rendering script is optional and uses NumPy/Matplotlib in the available analysis runtime. The manifest records all exact source/mesh hashes. Coupons remain separate from the nine production camera parts.

To generate only the optional inherited J4 comparator pair, run `make_coupons.py --inherited-joint-only`. It writes to `coupons/inherited-joint/` and does not replace the nine selected coupon pieces. Run `check_coupon_meshes.py --folder coupons/inherited-joint` on that output. Those optional meshes must pass the same checks before slicing; they are not included as already-verified selected coupons.
