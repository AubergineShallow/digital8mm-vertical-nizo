# Concealed vent structural supplement

This supplement updates mass and quasi-static load sensitivity for the selected shadow-plenum compact Kowa candidate. It uses the retained export18 section models and the current manifest. It is not FEA, a drop rating, a physical strength result, or permission to print/energize. All 56 inherited physical gates and M1 remain open.

**PRINT HOLD:** all four plenum edge mouths and the roof need slicer, bridge and cleanout evidence from current source-bound coupons; none exists yet. Selected geometry metadata: shadow-gap plenum trial4 (windowed flow ribs, supported stepped front mouth, cold-mouth lintels, skin bed chamfer). Roof and beam checks are geometric screens; they do not show actual toolpaths or bridge quality.

## Retained sections under changed load

With the same equal tongue sharing and local strip assumptions, J4 nominal lip/head stresses become 8.98/9.92 MPa, a 5.48% increase. The 30–70% station-sharing sensitivity multiplies these by 0.6–1.4. Joint contact, local notches, anisotropy, wear and creep are not solved. Grip gross nominal stresses become 1.59/1.69 MPa, a 4.40% increase; tub/floor compliance and joint slip remain excluded.

FP-11c thickened the carrier frame to its continuous 22.2 bed face and opened its counterbores, so the carrier payload rises by 4.67 g to 127.27 g. The reinforced arm section is unchanged, so its retained 5g, half-reaction screen scales with payload to 3.52 MPa and 0.414 mm (export18: 3.39 MPa, 0.399 mm) at assumed E=1600 MPa. This does not qualify the arm, boss roots, screws or service pulls. Current lens/collar mass is included in the manifest free body; preload cautions, FPS-rest load cases and 100 N strap comparison inputs remain inherited assumptions; thermal and physical tests remain necessary.

The old 0.90 N·m per-front-joint coupon target does not cover the new equal-share floor moment plus the retained 12 g-at-Z100 wiring-location sensitivity. The calculated minimum is 0.9366 N·m; use 0.95 N·m as the rounded proposed equal-share screening target after fixture review. A 70% station reaction with that same wiring sensitivity would be 1.3112 N·m and is a separate unqualified case. Existing C08/C09 geometry can screen the retained local mating surfaces; it omits the rear joint, independent lock and full tub. Do not turn a local coupon result into a whole-camera rating.

The unchanged catch-window and first-contact rules now cover separately reported feasible sampled tilts, yaw, translations and coupled poses; this is not exhaustive tolerance or withdrawal proof. Actual engaged height, dry seating, adhesive flow, bond, torque and creep require separate qualification.

A separate 2-piece bonded-catch study is generated from the selected tub print stock and the exact production insert: catch-host-coupon: 20.0 x 24.0 x 63.6 mm, -Y down; catch-insert: 6.3 x 12.0 x 15.0 mm, -Z down. Cropped host and fixture omit whole-tub compliance and complete lens/camera constraints, so no whole-tub 0.5 N m torque qualification follows. The study is additional to the retained structural and plenum coupons, with no production-part count change. It has not been sliced, printed, bonded or mechanically tested. See [the local coupon instructions](bonded-catch-coupon/README.md) for source identity and test limits.

Retain the current broad-face starting poses and 0.20 mm layer baseline: tub −Y face down, panel/carrier +Y face down, grip +Z top down and collar +X front down.
