## Claims that check out

- Report counts match the JSON: main 292/1/18 (only fail `pi5/cooler` 79.902671 mm3), plenum 49/0/21, HDMI 6/0,
  FPS 332 checks / 0 hits + 9 diagnostics, power 201 poses / 659 queries / 0 hits.
- Header map is unchanged and geometrically consistent: QT 1/3/5/6, 18/24 33/34, run 37/39. Probe of the source
  rows: pin 1 x -42.27, column pitch 2.54, pin 33 x -82.91, pin 37 x -87.99, pin 39 x -90.53; 5/6 share a column.
- I2C: encoder 0x37 (A0 bridged), X1203 gauge 0x36. GPIO6/GPIO16 stay reserved (inherited). Software and
  electronics folders are byte-unchanged from d38468e (`git diff --stat` empty), so W-6 power-key semantics
  (logind ignore, 2 s hold daemon, POWER_OFF_ON_HALT=1) are inherited. J2 is in parallel with the onboard button,
  so the electrical semantics are the same.
- Part identities are real: 1505 = 16 mm NO momentary, 29.4 mm max length, no rating published; 3556 = straight
  micro-HDMI plug 26.5 x 17.3 x 4; 3557 = R-bend micro-HDMI plug 17.5 x 15 x 14.5; 3562 = 30 cm x 10 mm ribbon;
  4448 = USB-A plug to 4 female jumpers, 30 cm; Pololu 2180 = RCY female, 2181 = male, 20 AWG, 10 cm;
  PHD-USSD-500G = USB 3.2 Gen 2, 34 x 15 x 7 mm incl. cap, 500/600 MB/s, no Linux listed, current not published;
  CK1044 = 1 pole 12 way, 6.35 x 38 flat shaft, PCB; SC1432 = Pi 5 8 GB; PRC SW-16R = over 250 x 0.08 mm, OD 3.0.
- Arithmetic is right for its inputs: 24 solder joints; 11 harnesses; 15 cable inputs; drop 0.129789 V cold and
  0.142811 V at 60 C (13.17 and 21 mohm/m, fuse 4.46, XT30 1 mohm, 8.6 A).
- Power last is kept: pack mated last (ASSEMBLY s8), battery isolation first in every service route (s1, s5).

## Probe results used below

- `drop_probe.py`: PRC strand area 252 x 0.08 mm = 1.2667 mm2, so 13.61 mohm/m before lay. 18 AWG fine strand
  150 x 0.08 = 0.754 mm2, 22.87 mohm/m (pack lead strand count is unknown; assumption). At 60 C: lay 1.00 gives
  0.1474 V (+2.6 mV); lay 1.02 gives 0.1494 V (+0.6 mV); lay 1.03 gives 0.1504 V (-0.4 mV). Hot fuse +10/20/30 %
  (assumed range, not data) gives -3.3/-7.1/-10.9 mV. Same model meets 0.150 V only with a shorter path.
- `slack_probe.py` (loads `routes.py`, CAD-free): QT main route + longest branch (27.67 mm). Over the solver's own
  bound (low 34..50) the shortest variant is 237.2..242.3 mm depending on qt_x; at the code's qt_x (-45.4) it is
  about 239 mm, i.e. under 1 mm slack at the 240 mm minimum lead and about 11 mm at nominal 250. Run lead minimum
  routes 214.3 mm (pin 37) and 209.5 mm (pin 39); the recorded routes are stretched to exactly 250.000 mm.
- `bomcheck.log`: `make_bom_vertical.py --check` under run_locked on this Windows machine exits 1, "Stale output:
  BOM-VERTICAL.md". `bom_diff_probe.py` (UTF-8 read) shows both files identical. Cause: `path.read_text()` with no
  encoding uses cp1252 here and the file contains U+2019.

## Other suggestions

- Generate the candidate wiring tables from code (as r7 did with make_tables.py), so BOM, WIRING and ASSEMBLY cannot drift.
- Port the r7 electrical checks to the vertical geometry: cable_routes (slack with allowance), mate_reach (minimum
  lengths), lead_access pigtail_drop (limit row), cable_stow, header_housings, completeness.
- Solder a 2-pin 2.54 mm header into J2 and use a socket half instead of wire joints on the Pi; it removes two Pi
  solder joints and makes the Pi side replaceable (check height against the cooler first).
- Put the fuse rating, wire gauge and polarity on a small label at the XT30 female.
