## Verdict

**0 BLOCKER. Latent: 7 MAJOR, 4 MINOR. Existing holds confirmed: 4 (2 MAJOR, 2 MINOR). 8 CLEARED.** The candidate can likely be built,
but not in the written order. Step 2 cannot be done as written: with the camera in, the seated centring gauge overlaps
the C-CS adapter by 927.75 mm3 (PHY-1). The microSD "panel-off" route is blocked by the lens collar, which the
`microsd_out` check leaves out (PHY-2). The main audit has none of r7's physical-assembly checks (driver, mate_paths,
mate_reach, handling, j7_float, roll_catch, lead_access...): 161 of its 292 passes are file/hash bookkeeping (PHY-3).
The camera FPC has no stated camera-end mating moment and no slack store (PHY-4). The FPS switch/panel sequence is
self-contradictory (PHY-5). The USB 5 V donor head ends 2.72 mm from the rear wall, so a normal axial cable exit cannot
bend (PHY-7). Step 4 needs more than two hands (PHY-8).
Good news, computed here: all 11 screws pass the r7 straight-driver envelope at their own step (C-1). The J7-R float
holds at the exported pose, if the lens is turned back off the tine (C-2, PHY-9). The in-place EVF HDMI push-up has a
clear column and hand corridor, so r7's BX-1 fix survives in principle (C-3).

## Notes

- Method: exact cadquery booleans and BRep distances against the saved STEP files of out/compact-kowa_lm6hc
  (manifest 53e668d4...). Corridors are boxes and cylinders; fingers are not modelled beyond box sizes. Scripts p1-p2
  ran. p3.py (exact FPC-space booleans) is in the folder but never got the shared CAD lock; it was stopped while waiting.
  PHY-4's slack statement therefore uses manifest bounding boxes, so treat it as an envelope result.
- Not checked: FPC route detail (unplaced in the candidate), FPS harness hand coordination (needs a trial), real COTS
  sizes (all proxies), the original-storage and Fujinon variants, upper stack screws vs the real Active Cooler.
- Suggestion: before any fix round, freeze a candidate step list as data (like r7 INSERTIONS/MATE_POSES) and generate
  the guide text and the step-present sets for the driver, mate and handling checks from that one list. Most latent
  findings here (PHY-1, -4, -5, -6) are order errors that such a list would have caught.
