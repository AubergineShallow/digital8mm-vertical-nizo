# Received-part measurements to close geometry holds

This is a de-energized measurement plan, not an assembly or power-on release. Remove USB/bench power and physically isolate the battery before touching internal hardware. Use ESD precautions, a caliper/depth gauge and scaled orthogonal photographs; do not probe energized contacts. Record part numbers/revisions, measurement uncertainty and photographs with the same datums used in CAD. Never force a mating connector or pull a ribbon to operate its latch.

This checklist now applies to the concealed-vent candidate: 158 × 63.6 × 100 mm compact core, cooler-side outer Y−35.6 / inner Y−25.5, plain 2.8 mm panel, and the retained straight unsealed CSI access slot. Export18 reports remain frozen references until rebound to this geometry.

For each item, archive the measurements, update the source/proxy geometry, then rerun its manifest-bound static and service checks. A corrected model does not close the inherited physical tests. All 56 physical gates and collar M1 remain open.

## Close-out record

For each changed interface, include a dimensioned sketch or annotated photo, actual part/revision, datum definition, instrument uncertainty, source-file revision and the new report/manifest hashes. Record any mismatch as a failure to resolve, rather than moving a datum, shortening a purchased cable virtually or deleting a contact to obtain clearance. Safe handling, actual hand coordination, print strength, electrical performance and thermal performance require their own evidence.
