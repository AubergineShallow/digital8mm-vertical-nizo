Option A Accept
Camera, collar and any lens change that needs a different collar go through the full module route: panel off (one J2
cycle), module STOPs, camera out. A collar change always removes the camera because the centring gauge needs the

Option B Redesign
B1: let the camera leave ahead of the carrier front, so camera service needs only the panel, as in r7.
B2: a centring gauge that seats without entering the lip, so a collar can be re-centred with the camera in place.
